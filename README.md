The Latin America and Caribbean Region (LACR) is highly exposed to a wide range of natural hazards, including hurricanes, earthquakes, floods, and droughts. Disaster Risk Reduction efforts in the region aim to strengthen resilience through early warning systems, preparedness, and sustainable development
The aim of this analysis is to gain a better understanding of the population potentially exposed to natural disasters in the Latin America and Caribbean region, both at national and regional levels, quantifying the degree of exposure of the population and  providing initial insights for risk assessment, emergency planning and risk reduction strategies.
